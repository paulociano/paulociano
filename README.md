## About me

I'm a developer, Food Engineer and financial consultant interested in turning ideas into useful digital products.

My work lives at the intersection of **software, artificial intelligence, product thinking and business**. I enjoy building applications, experimenting with AI workflows and creating reusable systems that make complex work simpler.

- Building practical digital products and prototypes
- Exploring AI-assisted workflows, agents, skills and automation
- Connecting technology with business and financial use cases
- Always learning, testing and shipping
